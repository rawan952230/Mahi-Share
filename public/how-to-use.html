How to Use MAHI SHARE

Send files

  1. Open MAHI SHARE on the sending and receiving devices.
  2. On one device, choose Create new room.
  3. Keep the room code and PIN available. You can also use the QR code when shown.
  4. On the other device, scan the QR code or enter the room code and PIN, then choose Join room.
  5. After the secure connection is ready, select files or a folder on the sending device.
  6. Choose Send selected. The receiving device must accept the incoming transfer.

Receive files

  1. Open Receive Files on the receiving device.
  2. On a supported desktop browser, choose a save folder if you want to save larger transfers directly to disk.
  3. Accept the incoming transfer when prompted.
  4. Keep both devices and browser tabs open until the transfer completes.

Important tips

  • Use an up-to-date browser and a stable network connection.
  • Do not share your room PIN with people you do not trust.
  • Large transfers may take time and can be affected by device memory, browser limits and network quality.
  • Some networks may prevent a direct peer-to-peer connection.
