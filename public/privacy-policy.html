Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to MAHI SHARE. This policy explains how our website and file-transfer service handle information.

Last Updated: November 13, 2026

Welcome to MAHI SHARE (https://mahi-share.onrender.com/). MAHI SHARE is a browser-based file transfer service associated with MAHI BLOGGER. By using this website, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

MAHI SHARE is designed to transfer files between connected devices. Depending on how you use the service, the following technical information may be processed:

  • Temporary room IDs and connection details.
  • PIN verification information used to establish a connection.
  • Device and browser information required for compatibility and troubleshooting.
  • Technical logs and server request information, such as IP address and timestamps, where provided by hosting infrastructure.

We do not ask users to create an account to use the basic file-transfer functionality.

2. File Transfer and Storage

MAHI SHARE uses WebRTC technology to transfer files between connected devices. File data is intended to travel through an encrypted WebRTC data channel whenever a direct connection is available.

  • The signaling server helps devices discover and establish a connection.
  • The signaling server is not designed to store users' transferred files.
  • Some network conditions may require a TURN relay, which can carry transfer traffic.
  • Files may be saved locally on the receiving device using browser-supported features.

Users should only connect with people they trust and should verify transfer requests before accepting files.
