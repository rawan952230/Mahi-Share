Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains, in general terms, how MAHI SHARE handles information when you use the website.

Files and transfers

MAHI SHARE is designed to transfer files between connected devices using browser-based WebRTC data channels. The signaling server is used to help devices find and connect to each other; it is not designed to store the contents of files. Actual connection routes can vary by network and may use relay services if configured.

Information processed

Room codes, connection messages and technical network information may be processed to establish and maintain a session. Files selected for transfer are handled by the browser and the connected devices. Do not transfer unlawful, confidential or sensitive material unless you understand and accept the risks.

Cookies and analytics

This website may use essential browser technologies needed for its operation. If analytics or advertising services are added in the future, this policy should be updated to identify those services and explain their data practices before they are enabled.

Third-party services

External libraries and hosting infrastructure may be used to operate the website. Their own privacy policies and terms may apply.

Children's privacy

MAHI SHARE is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents or guardians should supervise children's use of file-sharing services.

Changes and contact
