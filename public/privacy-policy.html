1. Introduction

Welcome to MAHI SHARE (https://mahi-share.onrender.com/). MAHI SHARE is a browser-based service that helps users transfer files between connected devices. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you visit or use the service and how it is handled.

2. Files and file transfers

  • MAHI SHARE is designed to transfer files directly between connected browsers using WebRTC where a direct connection can be established.
  • The signaling service helps devices discover and establish a connection. It is not intended to act as permanent file storage.
  • Depending on network conditions, a TURN relay may carry transfer traffic. WebRTC connections use encryption in transit, but users should only connect with people they trust.
  • Received files are handled by the receiving browser and saved or downloaded according to the browser, device, and options selected by the user.
  • Do not transfer illegal, harmful, confidential, or sensitive files unless you understand and accept the risks.
Important: We do not promise that every transfer will succeed or that files will be recoverable if a browser closes, a device sleeps, storage runs out, or a network connection fails.
